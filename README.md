## What is Solance?

Solance turns the top edge of your screen into a small, animated **notch**. Hover it and it opens into a tidy little control center; move away and it folds back into a thin pill that shows the time, what's playing, or a countdown.

No window to arrange, nothing to pin. It's made to feel like it was always part of Windows.

## Highlights

| | |
|---|---|
| **Music from any player** | Spotify, YouTube in your browser, Apple Music, VLC… Cover, controls, progress bar — and the accent color follows the album art. |
| **Synced lyrics** | The current line lights up as it's sung. Click a line to jump there. It also shows under the closed notch. |
| **Spotify tab** | Search songs, artists, albums and playlists, browse *your* playlists and liked songs, play, queue, shuffle (including Smart Shuffle) and change the volume. |
| **Focus timer** | Pomodoro with focus / short break / long break, cycles and a soft chime. Counts down right in the closed notch. |
| **Water reminder** | Glass and bottle buttons, a daily goal and an hour-by-hour chart. Gentle reminders during your hours. |
| **File shelf** | Drag files onto the notch to keep them close. Drag them out into a chat, an e-mail or another folder. |
| **Tasks** | Add a task, optionally with a time (`14:30`, `9h`, `+15`, `+1h`) and the notch reminds you. |
| **System alerts** | Volume, brightness, Caps/Num Lock, keyboard language, charger and battery, Wi-Fi/internet, Bluetooth headphones and track changes. |
| **A little companion** | When your PC is idle the clock gives way to a pair of eyes that blink, follow your cursor, doze off and wave hello when you come back. |
| **Overlay panel** | One shortcut (default `Ctrl+Alt+A`) opens every module full screen, on top of everything. |
| **Games friendly** | Choose whether the notch shows over borderless-window games and videos or over exclusive full screen. |
| **Yours to customize** | 8 themes, every color, fonts, sizes, corners, animation style and bounciness, monitor, which modules and alerts you want. |
| **English & Português** | Switch language at any time, no restart. |

## Install

1. Go to the **[latest release](https://github.com/LGueddes/Solance/releases/latest)** and download **`SolanceSetup-1.0.0.exe`**.
2. Run it. **No administrator rights needed** — it installs just for you.
3. Optionally tick *Start Solance with Windows* and *Desktop shortcut*.
4. Finish, and look at the top center of your screen. Hover the notch to open it.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 (1809 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit. Nothing else to install — .NET is bundled.
Developed and tested on Windows 11.

### “Windows protected your PC” — is that normal?

Yes. Solance is a new, independent app and isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows a caution screen the first time:

1. Click **More info**
2. Click **Run anyway**

You can check that your download is exactly the file published here. In PowerShell:

```powershell
Get-FileHash .\SolanceSetup-1.0.0.exe -Algorithm SHA256
```

Compare the result with `SHA256SUMS.txt` on the release page. If it doesn't match, don't run it.

### Uninstall

*Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Solance → Uninstall.* It will ask whether you also want to remove your data (settings, tasks, water log, shelf, Spotify sign-in). Your choice.

## Connect Spotify (optional)

You don't need Spotify to use Solance — music from any player works out of the box. For **search, your playlists, liked songs, queue and playback**, connect your own Spotify account.

Each person creates their own free Spotify developer app (about two minutes), so nothing goes through anyone else's account.

**[Follow the step-by-step guide](docs/SPOTIFY.md)** (with troubleshooting)

## FAQ & troubleshooting

<details><summary><b>Solance didn't start with Windows</b></summary>

Open **Settings → Behavior** and turn on *Start with Windows*. If it's on but nothing happens, open **Task Manager → Startup apps** and make sure *Solance* is **Enabled**. You can also re-run the installer and tick *Start Solance with Windows*.
</details>

<details><summary><b>The notch doesn't show over my game</b></summary>

Open **Settings → Full screen**. *Borderless window* (default on) covers most games. *Exclusive full screen* is off by default because some games flicker or minimize when another window appears over them — if that happens, run the game in borderless-window mode.
</details>

<details><summary><b>The shortcut doesn't open the panel</b></summary>

Another program may already use that combination. Go to **Settings → Full screen**, click the shortcut box and press a different combination.
</details>

<details><summary><b>“Lyrics not found”</b></summary>

Lyrics come from the community database <a href="https://lrclib.net">LRCLIB</a>. Not every song has synced lyrics yet, and the service is occasionally offline — Solance retries on its own.
</details>

<details><summary><b>Spotify says the Client ID is wrong / login fails</b></summary>

See the **[Spotify guide → Troubleshooting](docs/SPOTIFY.md#troubleshooting)**. The two most common causes are a Redirect URI that isn't *exactly* `http://127.0.0.1:5543/callback`, and pasting the Client Secret instead of the Client ID.
</details>

<details><summary><b>Smart Shuffle button only toggles normal shuffle</b></summary>

Spotify doesn't let other apps turn on Smart Shuffle through its public API. Solance presses the button in the **Spotify desktop app** for you, so the Spotify app has to be open on this PC. Without it, the button toggles regular shuffle.
</details>

<details><summary><b>My antivirus flagged the installer</b></summary>

Solance watches only the Caps Lock and Num Lock keys (to show those alerts), adds an optional start-with-Windows entry and presses a button in the Spotify desktop app — behaviors some security tools treat with suspicion on unsigned programs. It never records what you type. If it's blocked, check the SHA-256 above and, if you trust it, add an exception.
</details>

<details><summary><b>Where is my data? How do I reset everything?</b></summary>

Everything is in `%AppData%\Solance` (paste that into the File Explorer address bar). To reset, close Solance and delete that folder — or use **Restore defaults** inside Settings. A diagnostic log is saved as `log.txt` there.
</details>

<details><summary><b>Does it work on multiple monitors?</b></summary>

Yes — choose the monitor in **Settings → Size & shape**.
</details>

## Privacy

- **No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry.** Everything stays on your PC, in `%AppData%\Solance`.
- **Lyrics:** to find lyrics, the song title, artist, album and duration are sent to [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net). Nothing else.
- **Spotify (only if you connect it):** your requests go straight to Spotify. Your sign-in token is stored encrypted with Windows (only your Windows user can read it). Solance never sees your password.
- **Keyboard:** the app listens only for Caps Lock and Num Lock to show those alerts. It does **not** record what you type.

## Known limitations

- Developed and tested on **Windows 11**; Windows 10 should work but has had less testing.
- 64-bit only (no ARM64 build yet).
- The brightness alert only appears on laptops/devices that report brightness changes to Windows.
- Smart Shuffle needs the Spotify desktop app (see FAQ).
- Memory use is around 170–190 MB (it carries its own .NET runtime); CPU use is about 1% while music plays and near zero otherwise.
